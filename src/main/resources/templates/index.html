What is this tool

(insert tool name here) is a project directed by the center for digital services to help people upskill in various digital skills. Small government teams in Wales do not have the luxury of dedicated teams so we offer regular teams the chance to assess and train themselves to have all the skills they need combined.

How to use this tool

This tool is designed around self assessment. You will take quizzes for various "outcomes". These are related to tasks that your team can do. Each of these outcomes has skills that make is up called capabilities. When you take the quiz you will be assessed on the individual capabilities and given an overall score for the outcome. Your results should show you what you can improve at and allow you to access resources related to those skills.
